# Plaster surface v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. This is the opaque plaster surface texture only; route cutouts and edge shadows are drawn separately in the app. The app UI and runtime code were not changed for this material-generation request.

## Final prompt

+Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: one production-ready seamless material texture for a Swift iOS map's opaque excavation covering, square 2048 x 2048 pixels.
Primary request: create only the flat ivory-white mineral plaster SURFACE material, filling the entire image edge to edge. Top-down orthographic scan of pristine dry lime plaster / fine chalky gypsum, neutral warm ivory close to #F1EEE6. Restrained inorganic architectural material, quietly tactile, solid, matte, refined. Fine chalk grains, shallow tiny pores, very delicate brittle surface relief, a few exceptionally faint thin angular hairline fissures that are barely visible. The mineral microtexture should be discernible at full resolution but the whole image should look nearly uniform ivory when viewed small.
Composition: absolutely flat, no perspective, no object, no scene, no map, no UI, no excavated hole, no cutout, no torn border; material completely covers the square. It will be repeated in both directions, so all four edges must be seamlessly tileable with equal brightness; no edge framing or vignette. Even neutral diffuse illumination, no directional light and no baked cast shadows. Opaque background.
Constraints: keep brightness and hue extremely uniform; material detail is fine surface relief, not cloudy discoloration. Avoid fog, smoke, mist, watercolor, marbling, soft blotches, large mottled patches, dark flecks, grime, large cracks, peeling paint, visible paper fibers, fabric, dramatic creases, decorative pattern, distinct repeating motifs, lettering, labels, logo, watermark. Generate a single texture asset, not a mockup, not a contact sheet.
