# Paper surface

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. This is only the opaque substrate; the app computes the tears from route coordinates, not from a fixed overlay image.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable paper surface albedo texture for a real-time map mask, NOT a mockup. A full-frame perfectly flat sheet of subtly warm ivory uncoated cotton drawing paper, top-down orthographic macro scan. Very fine short paper fibers and delicate natural grain, extremely low contrast, quiet clean matte surface, faint warm off-white #f4f0e7. Uniform diffuse illumination, no directional baked shadows or gradients. Texture must tile seamlessly on all four edges. NO torn edges, NO cracks, NO stone, NO plaster, NO wrinkles, NO folds, NO stains, NO dots or dark speckles, NO objects, NO text, NO frame. The paper occupies every pixel opaquely. This is just a reusable fine paper substrate; the renderer adds tears dynamically.
